03

The blanks are not random

every distinct missingness pattern in the file, ordered by frequency

Nine columns can be blank. If blanks were independent there would be hundreds of combinations. There are twelve, and the top four cover 94% of the file — the gaps arrive in whole groups, which means they record something about how the measurement was taken rather than random loss.

06

Every numeric column, four ways

distribution · cumulative · percentile ladder · the whole series in time order

Eight columns from the file, plus two derived from it: speed_mph and step_m come from consecutive positions and timestamps and appear nowhere in the CSV.

Each card below is one column. The histogram shows where values pile up, the cumulative curve reads off any percentile, the ladder gives p1 through p99 explicitly, and the time series plots every non-null value in collection order so drift and run boundaries are visible.

Where a column has a long tail the histogram is drawn over p1–p99 and labelled with how many rows sit beyond it — the cumulative curve, the ladder and the time series always use the full range, so nothing is hidden.
